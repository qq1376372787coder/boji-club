## 0.15.6 · Feedback & Replies

- New "Feedback & Replies": submit issues/suggestions with photos and videos, track processing status and admin replies, unread-reply badges
- Draft saving and follow-up messages; attachments are compressed automatically — training/diet/health records are never collected and feedback is never sent to AI models
- Stability fixes and polish

## 0.15.0 · Visual Redesign

- Full visual upgrade: unified design language and design tokens — cleaner, clearer interface
- Rebuilt workout logging: set table, exercise cards, rest bar and edit panel all refined
- Fresh look for the training schedule, body dashboard and AI home

## 0.14.0 · Auto Follow-ups & Notifications

- Automatic daily analyses, workout interpretations and automatic weekly reports (enable in Club AI settings)
- Apple push notifications for daily/weekly report completion; reminders contain no sensitive data
- Stronger cloud-analysis consent: explicit choice at login, disable anytime — submission stops immediately

## 0.13 · Foundation

- 678-exercise Chinese library and the action system
- Membership and subscription system, with extensive purchase and sync fixes
- Ongoing AI service stability, throughput and quota improvements
- Multi-device sync, data export and account deletion matured
