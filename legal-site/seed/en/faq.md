### How much does {{site_name_en}} cost?

All features are available through membership, billed monthly or yearly. Prices and periods are shown on the App Store page — see the Purchase, Subscription & Refund Policy.

### Where is my training and diet data stored?

Account data is stored on our servers ({{storage_location}}) for multi-device sync; weekly-review body photos **stay on your phone by default** — uploading is entirely your choice. See the Privacy Policy for storage and cross-border transfer details.

### Are body photos safe?

Yes. Weekly review photos stay on your device by default and never enter the administration console; they are submitted only when you actively choose to upload them for AI analysis, and account deletion clears them. Please keep ID cards, addresses and other unrelated sensitive information out of photos.

### How accurate is the AI content?

AI output (plans, nutrition estimates, reports) may be inaccurate or unsuitable — it is for reference only and not medical advice. You can disable cloud AI analysis anytime under Settings → Privacy Notice and keep logging manually.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: submit a request at reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple ID or contact Apple Support; we can help with additional explanations. See the Purchase, Subscription & Refund Policy.

### How do I delete my account and all data?

In the App, open "Me" → "Delete Account", or email {{contact_email}}. Deletion removes your profile, training/diet/health records, AI reports and server media — see the Account Deletion & Data Deletion Notice. Tip: cancel your App Store subscription before deleting the account.

### Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later; iPad is supported, plus a home-screen widget. Health features require a device with Apple Health.

### How do I send feedback?

In the App, open "Feedback & Replies" to submit text, photos or videos and follow our replies; you can also reach us at {{contact_email}}.
