## 1. Not a Medical Service

{{site_name_en}} is a workout and diet tracking assistant. **It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation programs or emergency care.** Calorie and macro targets in the App are **estimated ranges** generated from the information you provide and your training frequency — not medical diagnoses, clinical nutrition prescriptions or treatment advice.

## 2. Consult a Professional First

If any of the following applies, consult a qualified physician or registered dietitian before using the training or diet features:

- Cardiovascular disease, hypertension, diabetes, respiratory disease or other chronic conditions
- Joint, bone or muscle injuries, or post-surgical recovery
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding
- History of eating disorders or current disordered eating
- Medications affecting heart rate, metabolism or fluid balance
- Recent surgery or other health concerns
- Under 18 or over 65 (extra caution and professional guidance advised for youths and older adults)

## 3. Training Risks

1. All exercise carries inherent injury risk. Assess your condition before starting a new plan; start low and progress gradually;
2. Ensure your environment and equipment are safe; have a spotter when needed;
3. **Stop immediately and seek medical help for any of**: chest pain or tightness, severe dizziness, breathing difficulty, palpitations, sharp joint pain, numbness, or worsening persistent pain;
4. Do not add weight or intensity beyond your ability; AI-suggested weights and reps are reference only;
5. Body-photo comparisons vary with lighting, posture and distance; trend interpretations are reference only, not a medical assessment of body composition.

## 4. Diet and Nutrition

1. Nutrition values — especially AI estimates — may be inaccurate; defer to actual foods and professional guidance;
2. Do not use App-generated calorie targets for extreme dieting or rapid weight loss;
3. Provide special-diet assessments voluntarily and carefully; for disease-related diet management follow medical advice;
4. If you experience loss of eating control, excessive focus on weight, or anxiety around food, seek professional psychological or medical help promptly.

## 5. Limits of AI Output

AI output is based on the data you provide and general models; it may be inaccurate, incomplete, unsuitable or inconsistent with current exercise science. **You must independently judge whether to adopt AI suggestions and bear the consequences.**

**In a medical emergency, call your local emergency number (e.g., 120 in mainland China) or go to the nearest medical facility immediately — do not rely on this App.**
