## 4. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary for the features below. If you do not provide certain information, the corresponding feature may be unavailable, but other features remain usable.

### 4.1 Account Information

| Category | Details | How collected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Login identity | Apple sign-in identifier (or phone number, if chosen) | When you register / sign in |
| Session data | Session tokens, sign-in time, account creation time | Automatically |
| Profile | Nickname; optional custom avatar | You provide it |
| Membership | Member & subscription status, benefit records | On purchase or benefit changes |

We do not keep your Apple identity token as a long-term identifier. Where the Apple token-revocation service is configured, refresh credentials are stored encrypted solely to check authorization and revoke it upon account deletion. Access tokens are stored server-side only as irreversible digests.

### 4.2 Body Profile and Training Preferences

Information you choose to provide: sex, age, height, weight, training goal, experience, venue, available equipment, weekly training days, session length, physical limitations, daily activity level, and an optional special-diet assessment (you may leave it blank or change it anytime). Used to generate training plans, nutrition targets and advice.

### 4.3 Training Records

Training date, exercise names, custom-exercise names/units and optional muscle-group/equipment notes, sets, reps, weight, completion status, training volume, plan vs. actual completion. Custom exercises are shown only within your account and synced online.

### 4.4 Diet Records and Meal Photos

Meal type and time, food names, calories and macro-nutrients, values you manually corrected, AI recognition source and estimation flags, and meal photos you choose to save. **Meal photos are personal data used only for logging and AI recognition — never for advertising.**

### 4.5 Health and Fitness Data

Only after you grant system permission (Apple Health or your device's health platform) and confirm each share in the App: steps, active energy, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep, workouts. We never read health items you have not authorized, and you may revoke permission anytime in system settings.

### 4.6 Weekly Body Review and Body Photos

Weekly reviews may include: weight, waist, body-fat rate and measurement source, fatigue/pain/difficulty ratings, diet-execution feedback, body photos you voluntarily upload, and photo notes. **Body photos stay on your device by default and never enter the administration console; uploading is entirely your choice.**

### 4.7 AI Inputs and Outputs

To generate plans, diet analysis, daily AI reports and weekly reviews, we send the content necessary for each task you initiate to the AI services: training records, diet records, body profile, daily health summaries, photos you submit, review questionnaires, and your Club AI chat messages. See Section 15 and the AI Services Notice.

### 4.8 Push Notification Identifiers

If you enable remote notifications: random installation identifier, Apple push token (APNs device token), current session identifier, and your notification preferences. Generic reminders via Apple APNs contain only the report date, type, account and internal report ID — **never your weight, diet, body photos or analysis content**. Delivery records are kept for no more than 7 days.

### 4.9 User Feedback

When you submit feedback: the text, photos or videos you attach, processing status and admin replies, plus App version, build number, OS version and device class to locate issues. We do not read your photo library automatically, do not upload your training or diet records automatically, and never send feedback to AI models. Please mask unrelated personal information before submitting.

### 4.10 Device, Network and Security Logs

For service security: IP address, request time, request type and status, failed-login counts, administrator audit records, service errors and task status. Used only for security, debugging, rate limiting and audit — **never for advertising profiles or shared with third parties for marketing.**

## 5. Sensitive Personal Information

Under the PIPL of the People's Republic of China and similar laws, the following are sensitive personal information: health and fitness data (including Apple Health data), body photos, body-profile and dietary information that may reveal health status, and personal information of minors.

Rules we apply:

1. **Separate consent**: when you first enable a corresponding feature (health sync, cloud AI analysis, body-review upload), we explain the processing, purpose and necessity on a dedicated screen and obtain your separate consent, independent of the overall consent, revocable individually;
2. **Necessity**: processed only to the minimum extent needed for the feature you chose;
3. **Consequences of refusal**: refusal does not affect other features — only the corresponding feature is unavailable;
4. **Minors**: personal information of minors under 14 is handled strictly as sensitive data (see Section 14).

## 11. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the service. Account-linked AI task text and results do not currently have a separate age-based deletion schedule; account deletion starts the applicable deletion process.

| Category | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Account, training, diet, health and review summaries | While the account exists |
| Local body photos | On your device until you delete them or uninstall the App |
| AI task requests, context, status and results | Stored on the server and linked to your account while it exists; not currently deleted automatically based on task age; handled through the account-deletion process when you delete your account |
| Original image bytes in AI task records | Scheduled cleanup removes inline image bytes after a task has ended for more than 24 hours; this does not remove related text, results or saved records |
| Incremental streaming-chat events | Up to 30 days; cleanup does not delete associated task text, results or saved chat messages |
| AI-generated training interpretations and reports | Linked to your account; handled through the account-deletion process |
| Push delivery records | No more than 7 days |
| Unused meal-recognition task photos (on device) | Up to 7 days; saved meal photos are unaffected |
| Account-deletion credential digests | Up to 30 days after cleanup completes |
| Service logs and security audits | As operationally and security necessary, generally no more than 12 months |
| Database backups | Retained under the backup rotation schedule and overwritten as that schedule expires |
| User feedback and attachments | Linked to your account; handled through the account-deletion process |

Account-linked AI task text and results remain while your account exists. Original task-image bytes and streaming events are cleaned up separately as listed above. Content sent to third-party model providers may be subject to their applicable privacy notices and API terms; the App cannot directly erase copies held by a provider.

**If a personal information security incident occurs, we will immediately activate emergency response and notify regulators within the time limits required by applicable law (typically no more than 72 hours). ## 15. AI and Automated Processing

1. **Consent and opt-out**: the login page offers an initially unselected consent control covering the Terms, this Privacy Policy, and cloud AI analysis of content you actively send; it must be checked to proceed. After a successful login we save that explicit choice only for accounts without a prior decision. You can **disable cloud AI analysis** anytime under Settings → Privacy Notice; submission of pending tasks stops, existing records remain, and tasks already sent to providers cannot be recalled instantly.
2. **Minimization**: each task sends only its necessary input; photos are submitted only when you initiate recognition or analysis; voice recordings are never uploaded (transcription happens on device where supported); your avatar is never sent in AI requests.
3. **Provider allocation**: tasks are allocated between DeepSeek and MiniMax by service status with mutual fallback; image tasks go only to image-capable services; legacy tasks follow the scope originally confirmed.
4. **Automated reports**: automatic daily analyses, workout interpretations, weekly reports and post-close follow-ups are enabled by you in Club AI settings; automatic weekly reports do not fill in your subjective feedback and do not modify schedules automatically; parameter suggestions require item-by-item selection, preview and confirmation.
5. **Content labeling**: AI-generated content carries explicit or implicit labels (e.g., "AI-generated") as required by law.
6. **Not professional advice**: outputs may be inaccurate or unsuitable — see the Health & Fitness Disclaimer and AI Services Notice; judge independently and consult qualified professionals.
