## 1. Overview

Paid features (membership/subscription) of {{site_name_en}} are purchased through **Apple In-App Purchase**, with RevenueCat validating subscription status and enabling cross-device restore. **We do not collect or store your bank card or payment account details**; payments are processed by Apple and its payment channels.

This policy applies together with Apple's Media Services Terms. Your purchases, restores and refunds follow Apple's rules first; nothing here affects mandatory consumer-protection rights in your jurisdiction (e.g., withdrawal rights under Korea's Electronic Commerce Act or cancellation-related rights under Japan's Act on Specified Commercial Transactions).

## 2. Membership Benefits

Depending on what the in-App membership page shows, benefits may include:

- Higher AI quotas and more AI features
- Automatic daily analyses, workout interpretations and automatic weekly reports
- Cloud sync and multi-device logging
- Seasonal growth and exclusive features and rewards

We may adjust benefits; **adjustments do not reduce core benefits during a period you have already paid for**, and material adjustments will be announced in advance in the App or on this site.

## 3. Pricing and Taxes

1. Actual product names, prices, subscription periods and display currency are **as shown on the Apple App Store purchase page**;
2. Prices generally include taxes applicable to your region that Apple collects and remits (as shown at checkout);
3. We may change pricing; changes apply only to subscription periods beginning after the change and do not affect periods already in effect.

## 4. Auto-Renewal

1. Subscriptions **auto-renew by default**: within 24 hours before each period ends, the next period is charged to your Apple ID payment method until you cancel;
2. **You may cancel anytime**; after cancellation the subscription remains active until the current period ends and no further charges occur;
3. When a subscription expires, is refunded or cancelled, membership features stop.

## 5. How to Cancel

Cancellation is managed by Apple (we cannot cancel for you):

1. Open iPhone **Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions** (or App Store → profile → Subscriptions);
2. Find **{{site_name_en}}**;
3. Tap **Cancel Subscription** and confirm.

Benefits remain until the current period ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

## 6. Refunds

1. **Refunds are handled by Apple**: submit a request at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) with your Apple ID, or contact Apple Support;
2. We can help you file or add explanations ({{contact_email}}), but **the decision and amount are determined by Apple under its policy**;
3. After a refund, the corresponding benefits end or are deducted;
4. Statutory refund/withdrawal rights in your jurisdiction (where applicable) are unaffected.

## 7. Restoring Purchases

After switching devices or reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple ID and tap **Restore Purchases** on the membership page; RevenueCat and Apple will verify and sync your subscription. If restore fails, confirm you are using the purchasing Apple ID or contact customer service.

## 8. Family Sharing

Where the App Store page indicates support for Family Sharing, family members share the membership per Apple's Family Sharing rules; grant and expiry follow that mechanism.

## 9. Gifted and Promotional Memberships

We may grant memberships through campaigns. Their duration, scope and rules follow the campaign page or customer-service explanations; gifted memberships are non-transferable and not redeemable for cash.

## 10. Minors and Purchases

Paid features are not directed to children under 13. Users under 18 should obtain guardian consent before purchasing; guardians can manage purchases via Apple's Ask to Buy. For minor refunds, contact both Apple and our customer service.
